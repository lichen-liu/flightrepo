## Blocking repairs for this research-plan scope

### B01 · Initial research focus contradicts the latest owner clarification

The recommendations put T01/T18 traveler purchase usefulness first. That was defensible as a scope-risk discussion before the clarification, but it should not remain the principal first experiment. Elevate an exploratory option-selection-analysis slice: find dated options, choose/pin several, explain tradeoffs, vary a constraint or next leg, inspect consequences and restore the initial plan. Keep cost, inventory, protection and personal transfer requirements visibly unknown; do not turn this exploration into purchase completion or remove those unmet needs from broader research.

Repair README, recommendations, the owner input record, Q07/Q10 and the validation sequencing consistently. The first comparison should answer whether a person discovers or understands a meaningful flight-option difference and can safely play with it. Evidence that can reverse the proposed emphasis: people cannot identify a useful difference, exploration repeatedly ends without a retained decision/insight, or commercial unknowns prevent even this bounded analysis. Do not count time spent animating the globe as value.

### B02 · “Includes these dimensions” overstates several V-to-U traces

Most V concerns are represented, but V18 reliability is absent as an explicit need/test. V21's computed counterfactual recovery is only generic repair/reasons in U24/T15. V06/V07's stay length, return and ordered multi-city question maps to U04 but only T18's ticket/booking test; it does not test the sequence itself. V09's local stay intent is weaker than a generic not-before bound. V03 group conflicting hard requirements and V13 geographic transit-country avoidance are likewise implicit.

Repair with substantive responses and tests or named explicit deferrals. A research register may defer any of these concerns; it must not claim the concern was fully covered because a broad neighboring U ID exists. Add a V crosswalk or structured field so a future auditor can check consolidation without interpreting prose from scratch. Historical reliability remains a secondary hypothesis; it is not an instruction to introduce a predictor.

### B03 · The clarified option-analysis task needs stronger experiments

Existing pin/sort and suffix-edit cards provide a starting point, but do not cover several meaningful alternatives:

- Reversible preview versus Apply: compare live hypothetical changes with an explicit committed result. The person must tell which is current, hypothetical and saved, then cancel without losing a selection.
- Multiple pins and dominance: compare readable side-by-side options, shared time lanes and a departure/arrival tradeoff display. Use one option faster but more stops, one earlier but with a longer wait, and a genuinely dominated option. Do not silently convert multidimensional preferences into “best.”
- Downstream intent: include a fixed later flight and a local stay length at a particular connection; test an impossible combination and show exact affected identities, waits and deadline consequences before commitment.
- Filtered network analysis: ask which alternatives remain under a date/carrier/via change. Test selected-context overlays against dense all-routes geography and topology; the counts/list/map must refer to the same filtered evidence.
- Counterfactual consequences: ask what changes if the selected next flight is later or the constraint is relaxed. Show a computed difference or an explicit unknown/incomplete result; a suggested recovery must not invent a candidate.
- Progressive depth: compare a concise initial option view with drill-down timing, identity, provenance and constraint explanations. Record whether useful facts are hidden or excess detail obstructs choosing.

These are hypotheses and comparison targets, not immediate implementation commitments. Name the fixture and failure condition for each before a session. Keep the all-three-treatment comparison and the unresolved entry/default decision.

### B04 · Some usability methods instruct the mechanism being evaluated

T09's method lists Face/Fit, switching and cursor actions; T05 names Change/Undo. Those can be implementation QA steps, but customer tasks must state outcomes first and allow unprompted discovery. Otherwise the test can prove a coached operation while missing the owner's usability question. Separate scripted functional QA from observed task attempts in T05/T09 and any new preview/comparison tests. Record when a control hint is actually supplied. Also distinguish a person's natural starting question from later interpretation probes that teach the desired boundary.
