# Research recommendations and alternatives

R09 · proposed comparison plan. Final interaction/default, customer demand and final visual direction are pending.

## Customer outcomes before screens

Travelers may need an affordable, purchasable journey that fits a real obligation and their party's requirements. Schedule exploration can answer only part of that decision. Enthusiasts may need dated discovery, geographic understanding and operating identities. Researchers may need defensible units, comparable coverage and reproducible evidence. These are distinct value hypotheses, including reasons people may reject Flyji; a mathematically valid route or attractive globe cannot prove them.

The latest owner direction centers exploring, selecting, analyzing and playing with options. The [first slice](first-slice.md) makes that one coherent loop. Public sources in [evidence](evidence.md) establish aviation context and existing patterns. The customer advocate's V01–V39 expand contexts such as already-booked later legs, real endpoints beyond airports, assistance and lost sleep. U01–U84 include these questions and explicit deferrals without treating a control as an established need. Additional questions can be added when a recent task contradicts the inventory.

These comparisons concern mechanisms, not new visual brands. Keep all three existing treatments comparable on identical facts, task difficulty, identities and error conditions. Do not create a final universal list-first, map-first or globe-first mandate from desk research. The readable list/time rationale is strongest initially for known-trip timing tasks, but it still needs testing. Geography and evidence tasks may justify another initial emphasis.

The globe can be playful and useful through leg focus, Face/Fit, explicit off-view recovery and event stepping. Its line/dot must remain an illustration of great-circle geography and a schedule. Flat maps, topology and named legs are credible alternatives, including for polar/date-line questions. User-controlled motion, single-pointer alternatives, keyboard access and readable equivalents are semantic/access requirements; pleasing motion is an experiment.

This order is based on decision impact and uncertainty, not invented demand percentages. Within a round choose a small number of questions and relevant contexts. A failed value test can lead to narrower positioning rather than more features. Different task winners can remain different representations. A required data source may be unavailable; that leaves the customer need unanswered rather than erasing it.

## Decisions and signoff

Q01–Q10 in [register.json](register.json) retain need/test/family traces and the strongest counterarguments. Both opposing roles must review the same final artifact bytes; an independent auditor checks provenance and scope. This can establish a defensible research plan and semantic requirements.

The entry model, final layout, disclosure wording success, traveler repeat value, actual transfer suitability, whole-process accessibility and public readiness remain pending. Agent agreement cannot sign off those empirical or owner decisions. Reopen a recommendation on conflicting observed behavior, an access failure, changed provider semantics, a routing failure or new owner requirements.
