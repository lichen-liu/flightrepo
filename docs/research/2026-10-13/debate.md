No verdict is presumed by this document. A substantive repair invalidates the affected whole-artifact receipt and requires a new review. The manifest identifies final reviewed files; initial agent messages carry their contemporaneous hashes. Historical product/design audits are preserved.

## Convergence rule and remaining dissent

Internal convergence means both opposing roles accept the current research plan and the independent reviewer finds no unresolved critical contradiction in its stated scope. Each factual objection must be repaired/checked or demoted to an explicit hypothesis. An empirical disagreement has a named observation/test; a final owner preference remains pending. No vote turns either into a fact.

Continue iteration after new evidence, rather than repeating identical agent arguments. Reopen on participant failures/non-use, unseen contexts, provider/routing changes, access failures or owner direction. Customer sessions, successful inclusive tasks, source-contract readiness and final design approval are still required for the corresponding product claims. Everything cannot truthfully be signed off in a desk-research-only round.
